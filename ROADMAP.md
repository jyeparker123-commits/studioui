# StudioUI Roadmap

StudioUI is a multi-modal AI studio for WordPress — image, video, GIF, music, and
AI chat companions in one white-labelled interface. This roadmap tracks where the
product is heading. Dates are directional, not promises.

**Live product & downloads:** [studioui.ai](https://studioui.ai)

## Model

StudioUI follows a **core-open, Pro-closed** model:

- **Free** — hosted credits, run out of the box, no keys required.
- **Pro** — bring your own provider keys and/or self-host for full control.

The open core gives developers a credible base to evaluate and extend; paid Pro
features fund ongoing development.

## Now

- [x] 15+ providers with pooled keys and automatic failover
- [x] White-label by default
